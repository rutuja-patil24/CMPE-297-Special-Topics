## Paper Summary

This paper provides a **comprehensive and cross-domain survey** of how AI agents coordinate across diverse environments — from robotics and traffic control to distributed computing, power systems, and LLM-based multi-agent ecosystems.

### Key Themes Explored

### **1. Core Concepts of Coordination**
- Cooperation vs. competition  
- Centralized, decentralized, and hybrid coordination  
- Communication protocols & shared knowledge representations  
- Task allocation & negotiation strategies

### **2. Coordination Techniques Reviewed**
- **Rule-based coordination**  
- **Game-theoretic methods**  
- **Distributed optimization**  
- **Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning (MARL)**  
- **LLM-based reasoning agents**  
- **Hierarchical and role-based agent teamwork**

### **3. Applications Across Domains**
- Intelligent transportation  
- Power grid management  
- Autonomous robotics  
- Distributed sensor networks  
- Social simulation & crowds  
- LLM multi-agent systems for reasoning and planning

### **4. Challenges and Future Directions**
- Scalability across thousands of agents  
- Safety, robustness and real-time coordination  
- Trust, explainability, and value alignment  
- Integration of LLM-agents with classical MAS  
- Multi-modal and cross-domain collaborative decision-making
